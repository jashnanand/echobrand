# 🎬 2-Minute Demo Video Recording Script
Use Loom or OBS to record your screen and microphone. Aim for **1 minute 45 seconds to 2 minutes**.

---

### Step-by-Step Recording Guide:

#### [0:00 - 0:25] The Hook & Problem (Rubric: Problem Understanding 15%)
- **On Screen:** Start with `http://localhost:3000` open on your browser.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"Hi Product Space judges! I'm excited to present EchoBrand Studio, our solution for Track 01: Media — Content Repurposing with Brand Memory, built with Wispr Flow. 99% of AI content is generic slop—banned clichés like 'delve', broken voice, and hours spent manually polishing weak English or redesigning graphics in Canva. We solved this with a voice-first brand studio."*

#### [0:25 - 0:45] The Brand Memory Vault (Rubric: AI Integration 20%)
- **On Screen:** Point to **Section 1: Brand Memory Vault**. Adjust the **Tone DNA sliders** (Tone, Cadence, Stance) and point to the **Anti-Slop Shield**.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"Instead of prompting an LLM from scratch, EchoBrand permanently anchors your tone DNA, formatting rules, and an Anti-Slop Shield that blocks corporate buzzwords before generation even begins."*

#### [0:45 - 1:10] Live Wispr Flow Dictation & AI Text Improver (Rubric: Wispr Flow 20% + AI 20%)
- **On Screen:** 
  1. In **Section 2**, click **"Demo: 🎙️ Hackathon Launch"** or click **"Dictate with Live Mic"** and speak a thought.
  2. Scroll down to **Section 3: AI Brand Copilot & Text Improver**. Click **"Fix Grammar & Polish"** or **"Viral Hook Booster"**.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"With Wispr Flow, creators speak at 200 words per minute. Even if your spoken English is rough or unstructured, our new AI Brand Copilot instantly fixes grammar, improves phrasing, and boosts the hook with 1 click—giving you human-grade writing in seconds."*

#### [1:10 - 1:35] 1-Click Multi-Channel Repurposing (Rubric: Prototype Quality & UX 20%)
- **On Screen:** Click **"Repurpose Across Channels"** (watch confetti!). Click through the tabs:
  - **LinkedIn Post**: *"Mobile-optimized whitespace and punchy takeaways."*
  - **X Thread**: *"4-tweet thread with real-time character counters."*
  - **Carousel Deck**: *"6-slide visual deck outline."*
  - **Newsletter**: *"Executive edition with A/B subject line alternatives."*
- **What to Say:**
  > *"In one click, raw dictation transforms into four platform-native formats simultaneously."*

#### [1:35 - 1:50] Advanced Canva-Style Image Studio (Rubric: Innovation & Creativity 10%)
- **On Screen:** Scroll to **Section 5: Canva-Style Visual Image Studio**. Switch canvas formats (`1:1 Square`, `16:9 Landscape`, `4:5 Portrait`), switch theme (`Sky Indigo` or `Emerald Mint`), then click **"Export High-Res PNG"**.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"No need to switch to external tools. Our built-in Canva-style studio instantly formats carousel slides into high-resolution PNG graphics with custom typography, badges, and color palettes—ready to publish directly to LinkedIn."*

#### [1:50 - 2:00] Closing & The Meta Twist
- **What to Say:**
  > *"We built this entire product using Wispr Flow dictation, and we used EchoBrand to write our official hackathon submission post. EchoBrand cuts content repurposing from 45 minutes to under 2 minutes. Thank you!"*
